# Wyvern Colors

Wyvern Colors makes it easier to tell your wyverns apart on Wynncraft. Reins and flutes show the mount's scale and horn colors in your inventory, hotbar, containers, and while held.

Reins have a horn-colored swirl and scale-colored trim by default. You can reverse those colors in the settings. Flutes have a scale-colored body with horn-colored details. Mystic reins also have a small sparkle detail. Renaming your wyvern won't change its colors.

## Installation

Use the download for Minecraft 1.21.11 and your loader: Fabric, Forge, or NeoForge. Put the JAR in your instance's `mods` folder and keep Wynncraft's resource pack enabled. Install only one version at a time. Java 21 is required.

This is a client-only mod. Nothing needs to be installed on the server.

On Fabric, use Fabric Loader 0.18.6 or newer. Fabric API is not required. Mod Menu and Wynntils are optional. See [COMPATIBILITY.md](COMPATIBILITY.md) for loader requirements.

## Settings

Open the settings through Mod Menu on Fabric, or through the Mods menu on Forge and NeoForge.

You can swap the reins colors and adjust the mount potential text. The opacity slider goes from 0% to 100%; 0% hides the text, including its outline and shadow. Text size ranges from 50% to 125%, with six positions to choose from. The default is 70%, Top Left.

Fabric's potential text controls use Wynntils. Forge has its own renderer. NeoForge uses Wynntils when installed and its own renderer otherwise. Debug logging is off by default and can be enabled for troubleshooting.
